Routes

How posture scans and inventory jobs reach the internet: Direct, or a named route (a custom proxy, Tor or Mullvad). The preferred route is the default for posture scans; a scan profile can pick another one. A scan whose route can't be used fails with the reason; it never falls back to a direct scan. Internal zones always stay direct. Changes apply to the next scan.

Tor the local tor client's SOCKS5; 127.0.0.1:9050 is tor's default

One exit for all scans: no per-host country with Tor. Exits block many ports and scans are slow. Private addresses can't go through Tor.

Mullvad a relay's SOCKS5 inside this host's Mullvad tunnel, per target

Automatic: the address's country, else the fallback, else any relay. Follow host: a relay in the address's country (DB-IP), else the fallback country, else the scan fails. Fixed: the country (and city) above. Inventory jobs use the fixed or fallback country (automatic without a fallback: any relay). A relay is picked at random among the active ones. Private addresses can't go through Mullvad.

Routes

Named routes posture scans and inventory jobs take to the internet: a custom proxy, Tor or Mullvad; Direct is built in. A scan profile picks its route (Default: the default route below). Posture scans run nmap under proxychains4; inventory jobs send their look-ups through the route and resolve names over DNS-over-HTTPS through it. When the route can't be used, the scan fails; it never falls back to a direct scan. Internal zones always stay direct. Applies to the next scan, no restart.

Scan limits rate limits for the public domain scanner

When on, the scan box skips the once-per-day, per-client hour/day, daily-total and queue limits, so a domain can be re-scanned as often as you like. Leave it off on an instance exposed to the public.

Internal domains looked up over your own DNS only

A scan of a name under one of these domains asks only the internal DNS servers below: no public resolvers, no certificate logs (crt.sh), no amass and no RDAP owner look-up. You get your own addresses and no internal name leaves the network. Only LAN addresses are kept (private ranges; public, link-local and loopback answers your DNS forwarded upstream are dropped). Applies to the next scan, no restart.

Host names are found by DNS queries to the servers above: a zone transfer when the server allows one, a reverse look-up of every address in these networks (empty = the /24 of each DNS server and of the domain itself), the zone's MX/NS records and common names.

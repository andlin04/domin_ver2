{# Users page: what each role and right means (checked against domin.access, domin.users and web.signin_gate). #}

Roles and rights

Admin
Every domain and feature, plus Operations, Settings and Users; the last enabled admin cannot be demoted, disabled or deleted.
Power user
Only the domains of its grants and the features its rights allow; without grants it sees nothing.
Maintainer
Per domain, includes subdomains: viewer plus that domain's scans, tags and schedules (with read-write on the feature); grants and removes viewer there for other power users (Domain viewers).
Viewer
Per domain, includes subdomains, read-only: inventory, posture, CVEs, changes and reports, as far as the feature rights allow.
Feature rights
Per power user and feature: none (the feature answers 404), read, or read-write (its changes); Inventory and Posture are read only.
Sign-in scope
Settings, Sign-in required for: Inventory (unticked: open to every visitor) and Posture (always a user; unticked lets only the lab sign-in-off stand-in in); the admin area always needs sign-in.
2FA required
Per role (Settings) or per user (Edit); not set up yet: each sign-in opens My account first.
